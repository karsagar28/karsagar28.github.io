  <h1>Working through networking questions in public.</h1>

  <p><strong>copy run start</strong> is where I write my way through networking questions.</p>

  <p>I’ll mostly write about Ethernet, data-center networking, AI plus whatever looks interesting enough to investigate next. This is also where I try to surface the <em>questions I was too afraid to ask</em> and remind myself that <em>thankfully, there are no silly questions</em>.</p>

  <h2>About me</h2>

  <p>I’m Karthik Sagar, a data-center network engineer at Cisco. My current work focuses on networks for AI and machine-learning workloads, including VXLAN EVPN fabrics, lossless Ethernet, RoCEv2, PFC/ECN, SONiC, and the automation around them.</p>

  <p>I’ve worked in networking since 2010, starting in technical support and product testing before moving into consulting, design, and operations. I hold active CCIE certifications in Data Center and Service Provider CCIE #40988 and a bachelor’s degree in Electronics and Communication Engineering.</p>

  <p>These are living notes. I expect to come back and revise them as I learn more.</p>
